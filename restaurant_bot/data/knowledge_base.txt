Welcome to The Gourmet Haven!

Opening Hours:
- Monday to Friday: 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 AM - 11:00 PM

Restaurant Policies:
- Reservations are held for 15 minutes past the booking time.
- Dress code is smart casual. No flip-flops or swimwear.
- We have a 24-hour cancellation policy.
- Maximum table size for online booking is 8 guests. For larger parties, please contact us directly.
- Walk-ins are welcome but reservations are highly recommended.

Menu Highlights:
- Starters: Truffle Fries, Calamari, Bruschetta.
- Mains: Grilled Salmon, Wagyu Beef Burger, Wild Mushroom Risotto, Vegan Lentil Shepherd's Pie.
- Desserts: Classic Tiramisu, Molten Lava Cake, Gelato.
- Dietary Restrictions: We offer gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free options. Please inform the staff.
